## How to read these

Three kinds of note, and they carry different weight:

- **field** — a category, scored on nine 1–5 axes in its frontmatter. The scores
  rank and provoke argument; they do not decide. A high score on a `low`
  confidence note is a hypothesis, not a finding.
- **play** — a business model built on a gap in a field: thesis, wedge, buyer,
  assumptions, and the kill criteria that would end it.
- **evidence** — a captured source, with an explicit note on what it changes and
  how much to trust it.

Every note states its own `confidence`. Both the field and the play here are
`confidence: low`, and the honest reading of the pair is in the field's closing
section: **a bad fit for a software portfolio and possibly a good business** —
`capital: 5` and `speed: 1` against `moat: 4` and `displacement: 1`.

All financial figures are desk estimates for evaluation and discussion — not an
offer, engineering design, or investment advice.
